Three steps to your first recording

Genesis explains camera and microphone access before you start. Auto-start is off by default.

  1. 1

    Pick a room

    Choose a studio from the library, or use your own picture. Each room shows a real preview first.

  2. 2

    Find your frame

    Start your camera, check the light and your microphone, and step in.

  3. 3

    Record or go live

    Record with a safety copy saved as you go. Going live is in beta.

Spaces for every kind of recording

Newsroom to classroom to podcast set. Pick one and Studio opens with it ready.

    What you get today

    Everything listed works now. Anything still experimental is labelled inside the app.

    Professional rooms

    Newsroom, podcast, classroom, office, library and more, or your own picture.

    Your name on screen

    Lower thirds, tickers, countdowns, scoreboard and QR codes in your colours.

    Run a whole show

    One Next button moves graphics, scene and teleprompter, even from a second phone.

    Clean sound

    Voice polish, optional noise removal, and music that lowers when you talk.

    Record safely

    Recordings keep a safety copy as you go and are saved on your device.

    A second phone as a camera

    Scan a code and another phone becomes a second camera angle.

    Privacy and honest limits

    Your picture stays with you

    • Your camera picture is processed on your device.
    • Recordings are saved on your device. Nothing is uploaded unless you choose to go live.
    • Use Studio as a guest with no account. Guest work stays on this device.

    Privacy policy and terms.

    What it is not

    • The background is a picture, so it looks best with the camera held still.
    • Older phones may need effects turned down. The built-in speed test tells you.
    • A phone browser cannot act as a camera for Zoom or Meet. Use Genesis to record or go live.
    • Going live needs a free relay set up once. We walk you through it.

    Help us make it better

    We are looking for creators, teachers, churches and small businesses to use Genesis for real and tell us what breaks. It is free during the pilot, and your recordings stay yours either way. Paid plans will be announced here before anything is charged.
